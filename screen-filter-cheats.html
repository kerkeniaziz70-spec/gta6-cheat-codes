GTA 6 includes screen filter cheats that change the visual style of the game, plus weather and time-of-day cheats.

Screen filter, weather & time cheats

What are screen filters?

Screen filter cheats change the colours and look of the whole game, like a camera filter. They are perfect for screenshots, videos and photo mode. All filters start with the same sequence (↓ ↓ ↑ → → ↑ ← ←) followed by two final directions, which makes them easy to remember once you know the pattern.

Weather and time cheats

Cycle Weather changes the current weather, Move Time Forward skips ahead in the day, and Pause Time of Day freezes the clock — useful to keep a sunset for your photos. The time cheats are unlocked by finding collectible Watches.

How to enter GTA 6 cheats

In GTA 6, cheats are entered on Jason or Lucia's in-game phone. Open the phone, hold L1 + R1 on PS5 or LB + RB on Xbox Series X|S, then input the sequence on the D-Pad. A notification confirms the cheat. Because all codes only use the D-Pad, the sequence is identical on PlayStation and Xbox.

Cheats cannot be used during the story: the Cheats list (Progress menu > Cheats) appears only after you finish it. Each cheat must then be unlocked by its own requirement (shown in the "How to unlock" column) — entering a code before that does nothing. Activating a cheat disables trophies, achievements and saving for that session — so save first. Full details in our step-by-step guide.

Frequently asked questions

Are there cheat codes in GTA 6?

Yes. GTA 6 keeps the series tradition: cheats are entered through the in-game phone with a D-Pad sequence while holding L1+R1 (PS5) or LB+RB (Xbox).

Do GTA 6 cheats disable trophies?

Yes. Activating any cheat disables trophies and achievements as well as saving for the current session. Reload your save to play normally again.

Do cheats work in GTA Online?

No. Cheats only work in story mode. Mods or hacks online break Rockstar's rules and can get your account banned.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version has been dated yet; we will add PC cheats when it releases.

Why is a code not working?

Make sure you are in free roam (not in a mission or cutscene), that you hold both shoulder buttons while typing, and that you have finished the story if the cheat requires it.
