Does the money stay?

Cheats disable saving for the session, so treat the cash as a way to have fun, not to progress your save. For permanent money, focus on heists, businesses and side activities.

Beware of scams

Never trust "money generators", console mod menus or sites asking for your PSN, Xbox or Rockstar login. They are scams and can get your account stolen or banned.
