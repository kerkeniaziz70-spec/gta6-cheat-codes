About Us

who we are and how we check codes

GrandCheatCodes.com is an independent fan website built to make GTA 6 cheat codes easy to find.

Our mission

We are GTA players who were tired of cheat lists hidden in long articles and videos. GrandCheatCodes.com shows every GTA 6 code at a glance, in a table you can search and filter in one second, on phone or desktop.

How we check codes

Each sequence is cross-checked against several published guides before it is added. When a reader reports a code that does not work, we re-test it and update the table. Pages show the date of their last update.
