How to enter GTA 6 cheats

Cheats are entered on Jason or Lucia's in-game phone. Open the phone, hold L1 + R1 on PS5 or LB + RB on Xbox Series X|S, then input the sequence on the D-Pad. A notification confirms the cheat. All codes only use the D-Pad, so the sequence is identical on PlayStation and Xbox.

The Cheats list (Progress menu > Cheats) appears only after you finish the story, and each cheat must then be unlocked by its own requirement (see the "How to unlock" column). Activating a cheat disables trophies, achievements and saving for that session — so save first. Full details in our step-by-step guide.

GTA 6 cheat categories

Player cheats boost Jason and Lucia: body armor, unlimited focus, no fall damage, parachute and drunk mode. Combat cheats give max or infinite ammo, no reload, explosive and incendiary rounds. Vehicle cheats spawn vehicles such as the Maibatsu Boomazoom, MTL Pumper, HVY Bulldozer or an ambulance, and can repair your ride or give unlimited nitrous. Environment cheats cycle the weather and apply cinematic screen filters like Cataclysm, Dollhouse, Faded Glory and Golden Hour.

Frequently asked questions

Are there cheat codes in GTA 6?

Yes. GTA 6 keeps the series tradition: cheats are entered through the in-game phone with a D-Pad sequence while holding L1+R1 (PS5) or LB+RB (Xbox).

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

A $250,000 money cheat is listed by several guides (↑ → ↓ ← ↑ ← ↓ →). Cheats disable saving for the session, so the money may not be kept permanently.

Do GTA 6 cheats disable trophies?

Yes. Activating any cheat disables trophies and achievements as well as saving for the current session. Reload your save to play normally again.

Do cheats work in GTA Online?

No. Cheats only work in story mode. Mods or hacks online break Rockstar's rules and can get your account banned.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version has been dated yet; we will add PC cheats when it releases.

Why is a code not working?

Make sure you are in free roam (not in a mission or cutscene), that you hold both shoulder buttons while typing, that you finished the story and that the cheat is unlocked.
