How to enter GTA 6 cheats

In GTA 6, cheats are entered on Jason or Lucia's in-game phone. Open the phone, hold L1 + R1 on PS5 or LB + RB on Xbox Series X|S, then input the sequence on the D-Pad. A notification confirms the cheat. Because all codes only use the D-Pad, the sequence is identical on PlayStation and Xbox.

Cheats cannot be used during the story: the Cheats list (Progress menu > Cheats) appears only after you finish it. Each cheat must then be unlocked by its own requirement (shown in the "How to unlock" column) — entering a code before that does nothing. Activating a cheat disables trophies, achievements and saving for that session — so save first. Full details in our step-by-step guide.
