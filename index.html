Quick FAQ

How do I enter a cheat on PS5 or Xbox?

Reports say to open the in-game phone with D-pad Up, hold L1 + R1 on PS5 or LB + RB on Xbox, then enter the directional sequence. A cheat must be unlocked first.

Do cheats unlock after the story?

Reportedly, the Cheats list appears after completing the main story. Individual cheats also require their listed challenge.

Do cheats disable saving or trophies?

Guides report that activating a cheat disables saving and trophies or achievements for that session. Save beforehand; these details are not officially documented by Rockstar.
