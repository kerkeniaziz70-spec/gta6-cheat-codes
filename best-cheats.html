Best GTA 6 Cheats to Use First

our top picks and the easiest unlocks

Which GTA 6 cheats should you unlock first? Our selection of the most useful cheats and the easiest ones to get.

Unlocked just by finishing the story

Four cheats are available as soon as the story is completed: $250,000 Money, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather. Start with these — no extra grinding needed.

Our top picks

  1. Body Armor — the most useful survival cheat (complete 100 workouts).
  2. Infinite Ammo and No Reload — unlocked through the Shooting Ranges; aim for gold medals to get both.
  3. Repair Vehicle — saves you after every police chase (win races at Gellhorn Raceway, Thrillbilly Mud Club and Boone Speedway).
  4. Parachute and No Fall Damage — great for exploring rooftops and cliffs.
  5. Hauners Rhino Tank — the ultimate fun vehicle (complete all Survival challenges).

Easy unlocks

Some cheats need fewer repetitions than others: Exploding Balls (hit 20 enemies with explosive damage), Vehicle Force (25 enemies with a vehicle) and Drunk (spend $2,500 on alcohol) can be unlocked quickly during free roam.
